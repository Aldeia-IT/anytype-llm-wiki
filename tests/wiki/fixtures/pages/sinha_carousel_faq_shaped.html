A capoeira school for families

We are a shaped full-time capoeira school run by the founder, who has trained for four decades and now coaches both children and grown-ups on six days of the week.

Every lesson mixes the movement with the berimbau, the Portuguese songs and the small rituals that open and close a roda.

“As the mother of two lively children I can only praise this place: they have picked up rhythm and patience, and they leave each lesson smiling.”
“Our son and daughter could not be more different, yet the coaches push each of them at the speed that suits them, and both feel safe there.”
I have no background in dance or fighting sports at all; is capoeira still for me?

Of course. Newcomers begin with the ginga and a couple of songs, and no one expects you to know a thing at the start.
