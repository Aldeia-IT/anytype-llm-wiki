A capoeira school for families

We are a shaped full-time capoeira school led by its founder, a capoeirista with forty years of experience who teaches children and adults six days a week.

The school is dedicated to the growth of the Afro-Brazilian art: movement, music, songs in Portuguese and the rituals of the roda are all part of every class.

“As a parent of three energetic kids, I want to recommend this school. Our children learn discipline and music, and they come home happy after every single class.”
“The school is a safe place to learn. My two kids are very different, and the teachers found a way to challenge each of them at their own pace.”
I have never taken any kind of martial arts or dance classes, can I still try capoeira?

Yes. Beginners start with the basic movement and the songs; nobody is expected to know anything on the first day.
