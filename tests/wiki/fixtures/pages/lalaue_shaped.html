Style: Regional

Background, Founders, Members, Locations and Affiliations

In the early 1940’s Capoeira was prohibited and anyone who was caught doing it would most likely go to jail. It was in this social context that Mestre Bimba opened his second (and most historically recognized) capoeira school in Salvador, Brazil with the goal of teaching Capoeira Regional. This school was called “Centro de Cultura Fisica e Regional da Bahia.”

The Center received students from the upper and middle classes of Salvador, and in 1937 it was officially registered with the state department of education, which made it the first capoeira school recognised by the authorities.
