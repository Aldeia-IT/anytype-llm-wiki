# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be respectful and constructive. This is a small, openly-published project — treat
everyone (maintainers, contributors, users) with courtesy.

## Expected

- Be kind, patient, and assume good intent.
- Keep discussion on-topic and technical.
- Accept that maintainer bandwidth is limited and responses may be slow.

## Not tolerated

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or demeaning comments, spam, or other
unprofessional conduct — in issues, pull requests, or any other project space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock, or remove contributions, and block accounts, that
violate this code, at their discretion.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to **j.h.scheufen@gmail.com**. For *security*
vulnerabilities, use private reporting instead — see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
